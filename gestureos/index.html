    Accessibility

    Tune it to your hands

    Tremor, limited reach or fatigue all change what feels comfortable. Every setting applies instantly and is saved on this device.

    • Snap to targets pulls the cursor onto nearby buttons and links so small targets are easier to hit.
    • Dwell to click clicks after you hover still, so you can use it without pinching.
    • Smart intent steadies the cursor as your fingers close, so clicks land where you aimed.
    • On-screen keyboard with word suggestions lets you type with gestures. Open it from the ⌨ button on the status pill or bind it to a gesture.
    • Right-click by touching your thumb to your middle finger; two quick pinches double-click.

    Browser extension

    Bring it to every website

    The extension runs the same engine in the background and drives whichever tab you're looking at: YouTube, recipes, docs, slides.

    1. Build it. In gestureos/ run npm install then npm run build:extension.
    2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
    3. Load unpacked and pick the gestureos/dist-extension folder.
    4. Allow the camera once on the welcome page, then press Alt + G or use the toolbar button on any site.

    What it can't do: browsers keep extensions inside web pages, so GestureOS can't move your system cursor, use other apps or run on protected pages like chrome:// settings and the Web Store. Controlling the whole computer would need a separate native desktop helper.

    Privacy

    Your camera never leaves your device

    On-device tracking. Hand landmarks are computed locally with MediaPipe running in WebAssembly. No video, image or landmark data is sent anywhere.

    No account, no analytics. Settings are saved in your browser's local storage, or in Chrome sync storage for the extension.

    Off means off. Stopping GestureOS releases the camera immediately, and your camera's light goes out.
