GGestureOS

Use any website with your hands.

Two quick steps: allow the camera once, then start GestureOS from the toolbar or with Alt + G.

1Allow the camera

Chrome asks once. Video is processed on this computer and never uploaded.

2Start on any site

Open a website, click the GestureOS button in the toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu) or press Alt + G. Hold a fist to pause and an open palm to resume.
